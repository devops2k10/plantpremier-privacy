Privacy Policy

How PlantPremier handles photographs, saved plant information, device permissions, and optional purchases.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

PlantPremier (the “App”) is an Android plant-identification application developed by Atanu Debnath (the “Developer,” “we,” or “us”). This policy describes data processing in the App, including its optional Premium features. For privacy questions, contact us at the address listed in Section 10.

2. Offline plant identification

PlantPremier analyzes photographs on the device using bundled AI plant-recognition models (OpenPlants ONNX and an offline fallback model). The App does not require a cloud plant-identification service or a user account to analyze a plant photo. Photos selected for plant recognition are not sent by the App to the Developer, the AI model publisher, Google Play, or RevenueCat for plant identification.

The App may use temporary image files supplied by Android or the photo picker while processing the selected image.

3. Information stored on the device

If you use the available garden and care functions, the App may store the following information locally on your Android device:

  • Saved plant names, identification results, photos, and personal garden entries.
  • Notes, watering dates, reminder settings, growth-diary photos or entries, and plant-health observations.
  • Corrections or feedback you save about a plant-identification result.
  • Local app preferences, feature settings, and locally cached information.

Garden photographs and records are stored in the App's local storage. The App does not operate a cloud garden account or upload these records to the Developer. Your device's operating system, photo provider, or enabled backup settings may separately manage copies or backups of files.

4. Optional Premium purchases and third parties

Plant identification works without an internet connection, but purchasing or restoring optional Premium access can require one. When Premium purchase functionality is enabled, the App uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to present products, process or validate purchases, check entitlement status, and restore access.

For these functions, Google Play and RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier, purchase tokens or transaction history, entitlement status, and technical information about your device or app session (for example, device type and operating system). This information is handled by those service providers under their applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not receive your full payment-card details from the App.

Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions and why they are used

  • Camera and photo selection: to let you take or choose photographs for identification or saved records, as permitted by Android.
  • Photos or file access: where supported or requested by the device, to open selected images and manage app-local saved photos.
  • Notifications: if enabled and permission is granted, to deliver optional local plant-care reminders.
  • Internet connectivity: for optional Google Play/RevenueCat purchase-related functions; plant recognition itself is offline.

Actual permissions can vary by Android version, device, and release build. You can manage available permissions from Android Settings. Permissions are not used to upload your plant photos for recognition.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your plant photographs or garden records. Local plant-recognition results, garden photos, and notes are not sent to the Developer for AI processing. Purchase-related information may be transmitted to Google Play and RevenueCat over their secure services as described above. No networked service can be guaranteed to be risk-free; device security and backup protection also depend on your Android settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Your locally saved garden items and associated photographs generally remain on the device until you delete them, clear local data through the App's Privacy & local data controls, or uninstall the App. You can delete individual plant records where the App offers that option. Clearing local data is designed to remove saved garden records, corrections, saved in-app photos, and local reminders; system backups or photo-picker files may be retained according to Android or backup-provider settings.

Purchase and transaction records retained by Google Play or RevenueCat follow their own retention requirements. For an inquiry about information processed in connection with purchases, contact us using Section 10 and include only the details necessary to investigate your request. Do not email passwords or full payment-card details.

8. Children and sensitive information

PlantPremier is a general-interest plant-identification app. Users should avoid placing sensitive personal information in free-form garden notes or photographing private documents. AI plant-identification results are estimates; they must not be used alone to determine whether a plant is safe to eat, touch, or use medicinally.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if PlantPremier's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated text on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
